ReelGen privacy policy

ReelGen is a personal tool used by its owner to make short educational videos. It is not offered to the public.

Google data

ReelGen asks for the drive.file permission only. With it, ReelGen can create, read and delete only the files it created itself in the owner's Google Drive: the finished videos in the ReelGen folder. It cannot see any other files.

ReelGen does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising. Videos are deleted from Drive automatically a few days after they are published.
